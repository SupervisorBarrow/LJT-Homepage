## Personal Details

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, supervised by **Prof. Junxian He**. I graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree. My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. My research interests include **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**.
